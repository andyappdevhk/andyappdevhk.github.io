English

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android app DualCalc (the "App"), provided by AndyAppDevHK ("we", "us").

Data we collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. The App does not require an account, does not connect to the internet, and all calculations are performed on your device.

Data stored on your device

The App saves your settings (display position, auto-sum on/off, VAT rate, "keep answers" on/off, and language) in your device's local storage so they are remembered the next time you open the App. If you turn on "Keep answers after leaving the app", the App also saves your last expressions and answers on your device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

Device sensors

The App reads the accelerometer only to detect which way the phone is tilted so that the layout can adapt in landscape mode. This sensor data is processed on the device in real time, is not stored, and is not sent anywhere.

Third parties

The App does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking services, and does not share data with any third party.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

繁體中文

本隱私權政策適用於由 AndyAppDevHK（以下稱「我們」）提供的 Android 應用程式 DualCalc（以下稱「本應用程式」）。

我們收集的資料

我們不會收集、儲存、傳送或分享任何個人資料。本應用程式不需要註冊帳號，也不會連上網路，所有計算都在你的手機上完成。

儲存在你手機上的資料

本應用程式會把你的設定（顯示器位置、自動加總開關、VAT 稅率、「保留答案」開關和語言）存在手機的本機儲存空間，方便下次開啟時沿用。如果你開啟「離開 app 後保留答案」，本應用程式也會把你最後的算式和答案存在你的手機上。這些資料不會離開你的手機，解除安裝本應用程式後即會刪除。

裝置感測器

本應用程式只會讀取加速度感測器，用來判斷手機向哪一邊傾斜，以便在橫放時調整版面。感測器資料只在手機上即時處理，不會儲存，也不會傳送到任何地方。

第三方

本應用程式沒有廣告、數據分析或追蹤服務，也不會與任何第三方分享資料。

兒童

本應用程式不會刻意向任何人（包括兒童）收集資料。

政策變更

如果本政策有更新，我們會修改本頁上方的日期。
